## 💡 Study Tips

### 1. **Type, Don't Copy-Paste**
Your fingers need to learn the patterns. Type every example.

### 2. **Build Projects Outside the Course**
After each section, build something YOU want (not just the exercises).

### 3. **Take Notes**
Keep a personal "cheat sheet" of concepts you forget.

### 4. **Use the Browser Dev Tools**
Press F12 constantly. Inspect everything.

### 5. **Join Communities**
- **Reddit:** r/webdev, r/learnprogramming
- **Discord:** Reactiflux, Next.js
- **Twitter/X:** Follow frontend devs

### 6. **Don't Get Stuck Too Long**
If stuck for more than 30 minutes, ask for help or move on.

### 7. **Review Regularly**
Every Sunday, review what you learned that week.

## ❓ FAQ

### "Can I skip modules?"
**No.** Each module builds on the previous ones. Skipping = shaky foundation.

### "What if I don't understand something?"
1. Re-read the lesson
2. Google the concept
3. Ask in communities
4. Move on and come back later (sometimes things click later)

### "How do I know I'm ready to move on?"
If you can complete the module project without looking at solutions, you're ready.

### "Should I memorize everything?"
**No!** Understanding > memorization. You'll have Google and docs forever.

### "What if I'm falling behind the timeline?"
**Don't stress.** The timeline is a guide, not a rule. Go at your own pace. Quality > speed.
